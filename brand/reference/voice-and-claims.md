# Voice and Claims Reference

## Current Messaging, V3.3

Approved update: October 26, 2026. Reviewed: 2026-10-27. This section overrides conflicting category, voice, or production claims in the background below.

Lead with AI-driven ABM and define it: your AI researches and writes; Folloze turns it into campaigns for accounts across the funnel, using a governed experience that adapts by account, persona, and stage and captures engagement to guide the next move.

- Promise "feels one-to-one" or "one-to-one impact at segment-level effort."
- Never promise "1:1 at scale," "1:1 for every account," "one-to-one at scale," "unlimited," or "without limits."
- One-to-one may describe one named-account build, such as a deal room.
- AI writes it. Folloze makes it a campaign. Do not imply AI builds the campaign alone.
- Microsite names an output. Do not describe Folloze as a landing page builder.
- Include engagement signal whenever explaining how it works: who engaged, with what, for how long, from which channel, and what to send next.
- Supported signals feed sales, MAP, CRM, and the next campaign. Do not imply every visitor is identified or every integration is enabled.
- Build. Activate. Signal. remains the capability register. Research, Deploy, Learn describes the marketer's workflow.
- Add Field marketing and CEO to brief personas; keep unapproved trigger assumptions provisional.
- Keep activation layer, agent names, headless, and open architecture out of general external copy.
- The Customer Proof Library remains v1.1. This update grants no new permission for customer names, numbers, quotes, or logos.
- Start visuals with one primary headline. No eyebrow, headline, and explanatory subtitle stack or leading-zero visual numbering.
- Do not use em dashes.

## Voice

- Direct and clear.
- Action-oriented.
- Conversational, but professional.
- Specific, not abstract.
- Strategic when speaking to VP and C-level buyers.

## Phrases to favor

- AI orchestration
- Autonomous marketing with governance
- From prompt to pipeline
- Campaigns that improve themselves
- Revenue visibility
- Scale without headcount

## Phrases to avoid

- Buyer experience platform
- Revolutionary
- Cutting-edge
- Set it and forget it
- Generator AI
- ABX platform as the primary category label
- Any claim of full autonomy without review

## Approved claims

- 50% faster campaign builds
- 10x engagement
- $6.3M attributed pipeline
- 5x faster campaign creation
- Engagement-based optimization
- Revenue attribution visibility

## Message discipline

- Every asset should tie to Scale, Engage, or Impact.
- Show proof quickly.
- Avoid corporate jargon and buzzwords.
- Keep sentences tight and easy to scan.
