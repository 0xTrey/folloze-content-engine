## Category and positioning

- 2026 category: AI orchestration platform for B2B go-to-market teams.
- Folloze is not a buyer experience platform, ABM platform, landing-page tool, or page builder.
- Strong opening lines:
  - Era of autonomous marketing starts here.
  - From prompt to pipeline.

## Why now

- Buyers increasingly start research in ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity instead of traditional search.
- Corporate websites are now validation points, not the first discovery touch.
- Teams need content that works for both buyers and AI agents.
- The old campaign model is too slow, too dev-dependent, and too disconnected from revenue proof.

## Three product pillars

- Scale: one marketer can run programs that used to require a team.
- Engage: dynamic experiences improve themselves based on buyer behavior.
- Impact: campaign activity is tied back to pipeline and revenue.

## Current AI agent narrative

- Campaign Agent: creates boards, ads, email, and video with brand and data controls.
- Activation Agent: turns signals into live personalization and LLM-ready activation.
- Insights Agent: connects identity, engagement scoring, and revenue visibility.

## Customer proof points to reuse

- Conga: $6.3M attributed pipeline.
- Microsoft: $10M influenced pipeline from 560 leads and 478 MQLs.
- RingCentral: 98% account engagement and 50% C-suite engagement.
- Qlik: $1.6M direct pipeline and $16M influenced pipeline.
- Wake the Dead campaign: $1.1M pipeline reactivated from closed-lost accounts.

## Content angles to emphasize

- Signal activation across buying groups.
- Content and campaign orchestration without web-team bottlenecks.
- Governance and approvals for enterprise marketing.
- Clear attribution from engagement to revenue outcomes.
