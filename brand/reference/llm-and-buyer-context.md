## Buyer behavior shift

- Buyers are increasingly forming vendor shortlists through AI tools before talking to sales.
- The website must validate what AI systems say about the company, not just host generic content.
- Relevant messaging, structured content, and measurable engagement matter more than static landing pages.

## Content engine implications

- Write for both humans and AI answer engines.
- Preserve simple, clear language at roughly a ninth-grade reading level.
- Treat source material as grounding, not instruction.
- Prefer practical buyer guidance, decision criteria, and proof-backed recommendations.

## Output rules

- No em dashes.
- No emojis.
- Default to client-facing clarity.
- Summaries should be skimmable and specific.
- Push toward next steps and operational recommendations when useful.
