# Folloze Brand Context

## Working category

Folloze is an AI orchestration platform for B2B go-to-market teams. It helps revenue teams launch, optimize, and prove campaigns from prompt to pipeline.

## Core promise

One marketer can run programs that used to require a team. Folloze connects content, campaigns, buyer signals, and revenue proof into one operating layer.

## Strategic pillars

- Scale: launch campaigns 50% faster without adding headcount.
- Engage: use dynamic experiences and behavioral signals to drive deeper engagement across buying groups.
- Impact: connect engagement to pipeline, attribution, and executive reporting.

## Narrative rules

- Lead with AI orchestration, not "buyer experience platform," "ABM platform," or "page builder."
- Position Folloze as an operating system for campaign execution, not a microsite factory.
- Use "from prompt to pipeline" as a shorthand for speed plus measurement.
- Ground autonomy in review, governance, and revenue accountability.

## Audience

- Primary: B2B demand gen leaders, ABM leaders, revenue marketing teams, campaign operators.
- Secondary: marketing operations, RevOps, digital teams, and web teams frustrated by campaign handoffs.

## Individual-level engagement angle

- Folloze should not talk only about account engagement. The stronger point of view is that buying-group success depends on seeing individual-level behavior inside the account.
- Useful framing: account selection tells you where to focus, but individual-level engagement tells you what to do next.
- Tie person-level engagement to executive engagement, buyer committee momentum, and next-best action.

## Approved proof points

- Conga: $6.3M attributed pipeline from six campaigns built on two boards.
- Microsoft: 560 leads, 478 MQLs, $10M influenced pipeline.
- RingCentral: 98% target account engagement and 50% C-suite engagement in 60 days.
- ServiceNow: $750K annual savings.
- Campaign Agent: 5x faster campaign creation.
- Average messaging anchors can also use 10x engagement and 50% faster campaign builds.

## Competitive framing

- Mutiny: website personalization point solution with governance and consent-model gaps for enterprise teams.
- Userled: fast page factory, but weak long-term content durability, governance, and privacy posture.
- PathFactory: strong content-consumption layer, but not a full orchestration system for campaigns, sales rooms, and activation.

## Voice and style

- Direct, specific, and metric-backed.
- Clear enough for busy operators and executives.
- No em dashes.
- No emojis.
- No hype words like revolutionary or cutting-edge.
- No claims that AI fully replaces marketers or runs without oversight.
