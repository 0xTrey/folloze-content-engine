## Positioning Note

Folloze helps teams target and convert key accounts by turning content into live, governed, personalized account experiences, activating those experiences across channels, and capturing the engagement signals that drive the next move.

The screenshot uses the label `AI Buyer Experience Platform`. Current buyer-safe messaging should usually explain the function instead of leading with that category label: Folloze builds personalized account experiences, activates them across campaign, sales, web, and event motions, and captures first-party engagement signals.

## Campaign Builder

The campaign builder is the no-code workspace for creating, publishing, and activating Folloze experiences. In product language, those experiences are often called boards. In buyer-facing language, they are personalized microsites, campaign destinations, sales rooms, event hubs, resource centers, or embedded website experiences.

The campaign builder lets marketers assemble an experience from templates, sections, content assets, CTAs, forms, routing rules, personalization rules, access controls, and analytics settings without waiting on web or development cycles. It is the place where a campaign brief becomes a live destination that can be shared through email, ads, sales outreach, event programs, partner campaigns, web personalization, resource centers, or website embeds.

Core functions:

- Build on-brand microsites, landing pages, virtual event hubs, deal rooms, and resource centers.
- Use templates and reusable sections to avoid rebuilding every campaign from scratch.
- Add content items, forms, CTAs, videos, articles, and gated or ungated assets.
- Apply brand controls such as fonts, colors, logos, style rules, and approval workflows.
- Set access and security rules such as public access, password protection, 2FA, or SSO-controlled experiences.
- Create campaign links, tracking links, individual links, group links, and source-specific links.
- Capture engagement data at the board, account, person, content, CTA, and source level.
- Activate the same destination across account-based campaigns, demand generation, sales development, events, sales rooms, and website engagement.

What it is not: the campaign builder is not just a static landing-page tool. Its value is that the destination can personalize by account, source, persona, stage, behavior, or identity, then report engagement back to marketing and sales.

## Generator Agents

Generator agents are the AI creation layer inside Folloze. The current internal pricing and product language most often points to the Generator, Translation, and Video agent family: tools that help teams create, refine, personalize, localize, and in some cases generate video content for Folloze experiences.

The practical job of the generator agents is to get a team from a brief or prompt to a usable first draft faster. A marketer can define the campaign goal, audience, solution, region, industry, account, or persona, then use the agent to generate or rewrite board copy and section-level messaging.

Core functions:

- Generate board or section copy from a prompt, campaign goal, product, solution, and target audience.
- Personalize copy for target accounts, industries, roles, regions, buying stages, or campaign segments.
- Translate and localize messaging so regional teams can move faster while preserving meaning and brand voice.
- Refine existing copy section by section instead of forcing a full rewrite.
- Use brand voice controls managed by admins so generated content follows approved company guidance.
- Support video generation where the Video Agent is enabled, including avatar-led board intro videos and custom scripts.
- Use website, campaign, account, or content context as input where configured so the first draft is closer to the intended brand and audience.

The generator agents should be described as AI-assisted creation, not unchecked automation. Teams still review the output, align it to the campaign strategy, and decide what gets published.

Do not over-rotate on agent names in external copy. The customer-facing value is faster creation of governed, personalized experiences, not the internal taxonomy of every agent.

## Personalization Engine

The personalization engine is the decision layer that changes what a buyer sees based on who they are, where they came from, what account they belong to, how they behave, and what the company already knows about them.

It supports both rules-based personalization and AI-assisted content recommendations. In simple terms: the same campaign destination can show different logos, copy, CTAs, banners, contact cards, forms, offers, content paths, or recommendations to different visitors.

Core functions:

- Personalize content, messaging, visuals, and CTAs by account, domain, industry, role, source, stage, segment, or known identity.
- Adapt experiences by stage, industry, account, region, persona, behavior, and campaign source where the data is available.
- Support inbound and outbound motions through known and unknown visitor handling.
- Use progressive identity to connect behavior from anonymous, known, account-level, and person-level signals over time.
- Recommend content from the Content Center based on prospect behavior, account context, industry trends, or engagement history.
- Adjust experience paths as visitors engage with boards and content.
- Feed engagement signals back into dashboards, reports, CRM, MAP, sales alerts, and downstream workflows.

The personalization engine matters because B2B buyers rarely move through a single generic path. A CFO, practitioner, partner, event attendee, and late-stage buying committee member may all need different content, proof, CTA paths, and follow-up triggers.

## Enrichment

Enrichment is the process of improving account and contact records with additional data so Folloze can identify, segment, personalize, route, and report with more context.

Enrichment can include firmographic data, industry, company size, account domain, location, title, role, persona, buying group context, CRM fields, MAP fields, ABM segment membership, intent data, engagement history, and other attributes made available through connected systems or data providers.

Internal data strategy material breaks this into four practical data categories: account identification, account enrichment and contact identification, AI-assisted data enrichment such as account insights or business signals, and context or intent data that can help prioritize the right audience or message.

Core functions:

- Resolve or improve account and contact identity.
- Add missing account or contact attributes needed for segmentation and personalization.
- Support dynamic logos, account-specific copy, industry-specific banners, persona paths, and role-based CTAs.
- Help sales and marketing understand which accounts and people are engaging.
- Improve audience quality before launch and improve follow-up quality after engagement.
- Support account selection, audience segmentation, and campaign generation with better data than a flat uploaded list.

Enrichment should not be positioned as a replacement for CRM hygiene or a standalone data vendor unless the package specifically includes that scope. Its value inside Folloze is making experiences and signals more relevant.

## Account And Contact Research

Research is the intelligence workflow that turns raw account or contact data into useful campaign context. It helps teams understand why an account matters, what message is likely to land, what buying group or persona is relevant, and what content path should be used.

Common research outputs:

- Account overview and business context.
- Relevant industry, use-case, or initiative signals.
- Suggested messaging angles.
- Persona or buying group assumptions.
- Content recommendations.
- Sales follow-up context.

Research is especially useful for 1:1 ABM, strategic account programs, executive engagement, sales rooms, outbound motions, and renewal or expansion plays.

## Audience Builder

Audience Builder is the capability area for assembling, filtering, and activating target audiences from available account, contact, intent, CRM, MAP, ABM, enrichment, and engagement data.

The February 2026 pricing guide marks Audience Builder as roadmap. Describe it carefully: it is the product direction for giving teams a more direct way to define audiences inside Folloze, not a fully committed live entitlement unless Product confirms availability for the specific customer and package.

Expected function:

- Build target lists or segments from CRM, MAP, ABM, CDP, enrichment, imports, or engagement data.
- Filter by account attributes, contact attributes, buying stage, source, behavior, intent, or campaign membership.
- Route audiences into personalized experiences, emails, ads, sales plays, or nurture motions.
- Help teams reuse warm audiences from prior engagement instead of starting every campaign cold.

## Content Engine

The content engine is the governed content layer. It combines repository, organization, reuse, personalization, recommendations, and analytics.

At the center is the idea that campaign destinations should not be disconnected pages filled with one-off assets. Content should be stored, tagged, reused, updated centrally, recommended intelligently, and measured consistently.

Core functions:

- Store and organize approved content in a central repository or Content Center.
- Reuse assets across many boards and campaign destinations.
- Update shared content centrally so changes can propagate where the asset is used.
- Present native and third-party content inside Folloze experiences.
- Track content views, engagement duration, location, device, CTA clicks, and journey progress.
- Recommend relevant content based on account, stage, topic, profile, behavior, or AI insights.
- Support articles, resource centers, blogs, customer ABM content, webinars, videos, PDFs, decks, and third-party embeds where configured.
- Support AI-assisted content selection or content path recommendations where Content AI or Content Agent capabilities are enabled.

The content engine is what turns Folloze from a page builder into a system for serving the right content and learning from every interaction.

## CRM Integrations

CRM integrations connect Folloze engagement to the account, contact, lead, opportunity, and seller workflows that revenue teams already use.

The most important CRM job is not simply syncing a field. It is making Folloze engagement visible and actionable where sales and marketing work. When someone views a board, consumes content, clicks a CTA, or shows account-level engagement, the CRM integration can help route that signal into records, dashboards, tasks, alerts, campaign attribution, or seller follow-up.

In the Drive decks, the integration story is a loop: Folloze uses CRM, MAP, ABM, and data-provider context to build and personalize experiences; buyers engage with those experiences; Folloze sends behavioral analytics and account engagement signals back to the CRM, MAP, ABM platform, data lake, or sales workflow.

Core functions:

- Sync known visitor and engagement activity to CRM records.
- Associate Folloze activity with accounts, contacts, leads, campaigns, and opportunities where configured.
- Surface account engagement, individual activity, content interests, and source tracking to sales.
- Help sellers prioritize follow-up based on behavior, not guesswork.
- Use CRM fields for targeting, personalization, routing, or access control.
- Support reporting on campaign influence, account engagement, and sales follow-up.
- Return behavioral analytics and account engagement scores to connected systems where configured.

Named CRM and related examples in current public/help material include Salesforce and HubSpot. Exact CRM scope, object mapping, sync frequency, field direction, and supported customizations should be confirmed during implementation.

## Integration Landscape: MAP, CRM, CDP, And ABM

The package grid groups integrations into four common buckets:

| Integration type | What it means | Common use |
|---|---|---|
| MAP | Marketing automation platforms such as Marketo, Eloqua, HubSpot, or Salesforce Account Engagement, depending on package and configuration. | Forms, campaign membership, lead capture, nurture routing, cookie matching, scoring, and campaign reporting. |
| CRM | Revenue systems such as Salesforce or HubSpot CRM, depending on package and configuration. | Account/contact sync, engagement visibility, seller follow-up, opportunity context, and attribution. |
| CDP | Customer data platform or data-routing layer. | Audience ingestion, identity stitching, event routing, behavioral data, and downstream activation. Confirm supported CDP connectors before committing. |
| ABM | Account intelligence and intent platforms such as 6sense, Demandbase, and D&B. | Account identification, segment membership, intent signals, firmographic enrichment, campaign orchestration, and personalization rules. |

The integration value proposition is simple: Folloze activates the data a company already has, then sends engagement signals back so the stack learns from what buyers actually did.

## Global Scalability

Global scalability is the set of capabilities that make Folloze viable for enterprise, multi-team, multi-brand, multi-region, and governed rollout. In the February 2026 pricing guide, Global Scalability is included in Premium and Enterprise packages and is not included in Starter or Professional.

### Localization

Localization lets teams adapt board copy, campaign messaging, content, and experience structure for different languages, regions, and local market contexts.

What it includes:

- AI-assisted translation or localization of board and section copy where enabled.
- Regional variants of messaging, images, CTAs, offers, and content.
- Localized campaign destinations for field, partner, regional, or country teams.
- Brand voice preservation across language variants.

Localization is not just word-for-word translation. The goal is to keep the campaign relevant to the region while maintaining company standards.

### DAM And Governed Asset Management

DAM means Digital Asset Management. In this package context, it should be explained as the governed asset layer that helps teams use approved content and creative assets consistently.

Depending on the implementation, this may mean Folloze's Content Center, Media Library, external DAM integrations, or a governed asset workflow. The exact connector list should be confirmed before external commitment.

What it supports:

- Centralized approved content and creative assets.
- Reuse of the same asset across many boards.
- Central updates to reduce stale or off-brand content.
- Asset tagging, organization, and discovery.
- Governance for brand, legal, regional, partner, and customer-facing content.

### Data Warehouse

Data warehouse access gives teams a way to analyze Folloze engagement and campaign data outside of standard dashboards.

What it supports:

- BI analysis across boards, accounts, identities, campaigns, content, sources, and time periods.
- Deeper reporting than front-end dashboards when analysts need SQL, warehouse joins, or custom models.
- Connection of Folloze engagement data with CRM, MAP, pipeline, product, customer, or finance data.
- Executive reporting on campaign performance, engagement trends, and revenue influence.

The Data Warehouse capability is important for enterprise teams that need their own analytics models, not just built-in reports.

### SSO

SSO means Single Sign-On. It lets users access Folloze through the company's identity provider instead of managing separate credentials.

What it supports:

- Centralized login and access control.
- Easier onboarding and offboarding.
- Enterprise security and compliance alignment.
- Role and permission governance for distributed teams.
- Access control for internal processes, partners, customers, or protected experiences where configured.

SSO is part of the trust and governance layer. It matters most for enterprise procurement, security review, and multi-team rollout.

## Website Engager

Website Engager lets a company embed Folloze experiences or board sections into its existing website so the main corporate URL can deliver personalized content and capture buyer engagement. The current pricing guide uses the module label `Website Engagement`; public product material also uses `Website Engager` for the feature.

Instead of forcing every campaign to live on a separate landing page, Website Engager can turn the website into a personalized launch point for ABM, inbound, resource center, event, or sales motions.

Core functions:

- Embed Folloze boards or sections into a website.
- Use the website as a launch pad for personalized buyer journeys.
- Personalize embedded content by account, industry, role, source, stage, or behavior.
- Track visitor activity, content consumption, identity, and engagement from the embedded experience.
- Route interested visitors into campaign pages, content paths, meeting CTAs, offers, or sales follow-up.

Common use cases:

- Inbound website personalization.
- ABM campaign pages hosted from a main domain.
- Personalized resource centers.
- Industry or persona content paths.
- Event follow-up destinations.
- Offers, demo CTAs, or sales contact paths tailored by visitor context.

## Events

Events are Folloze-powered experiences for field events, webinars, virtual events, hybrid events, executive programs, roadshows, customer events, and post-event follow-up. In the February 2026 pricing guide, Events is included in Professional, Premium, and Enterprise.

The value is that an event can have one governed digital destination before, during, and after the event. That destination can handle registration paths, agendas, speaker content, session content, sponsor or partner materials, videos, on-demand replays, follow-up CTAs, and sales routing.

Core functions:

- Build event hubs, webinar destinations, session pages, and follow-up rooms.
- Host or stream video, webinar, and simulive content where enabled.
- Add speaker bios, agendas, resource tracks, forms, CTAs, and post-event assets.
- Personalize event experiences by segment, account, attendee type, region, or role.
- Capture engagement before, during, and after the event.
- Use event engagement to trigger sales follow-up, nurture, retargeting, or account scoring.

Events should be positioned as a buyer engagement motion, not just registration support. The point is to turn event attention into measurable follow-up.

## Creators Versus Collaborators

Folloze licenses and roles control what users can do. A user can have one license and one role at a time.

| License | Typical user | What they can do |
|---|---|---|
| Creator | Marketing, demand gen, ABM, field marketing, campaign owners, platform admins. | Build boards, assemble content, run campaigns, access system capabilities allowed by the company plan, and use engagement analytics. Creator roles can include Company Admin, Company Editor, Creator Author, Creator Player, and Creator Player Limited. |
| Collaborator Pro | Sales users who need deeper involvement in sales-oriented or RFP boards. | Add content to boards, change access and security settings, and preview the Designer. They cannot create, edit, duplicate, archive, or delete boards. |
| Collaborator | Sales users who need engagement visibility and sharing capability. | Be added as subscribers, access account and analytics dashboards, use Report Center, send email campaigns, and view board-level analytics where allowed. They cannot create, own, or duplicate boards. |
| Associate | Basic sales or contact-card users. | Cannot log in to the Folloze system. Can be added as article authors, inviters, contact cards, subscribers, and CTA notification recipients. They cannot create, edit, own, or send campaigns. |

In commercial packaging, `Users Included` must be explicit. If the package says "2 users included" but the order form later says "2 Creators included," use the clearer order-form language. Do not assume collaborator seats are included unless the package or order form says so.

## Folloze AI/Data Credits

Folloze AI/Data credits are a metered usage allowance for features that consume AI generation capacity, data capacity, or third-party-style intelligence capacity.

In plain language: credits are the pool that pays for high-cost AI or data actions so the platform can include a baseline allowance in each package and sell additional usage when a team needs more.

Likely credit-consuming areas:

- AI generation for board, section, copy, translation, or personalization workflows where metered.
- Video Agent usage, where video generation is credit-based.
- Account and contact enrichment or research actions where included in the Folloze credit model.
- Audience intelligence actions where they rely on paid data or AI processing.
- Additional AI/Data credit packs purchased as add-ons.

Important distinctions:

- The February 2026 pricing guide confirms included annual quantities by package: Starter 250, Professional 1,000, Premium 2,000, and Enterprise 5,000.
- The exact credit burn table must be confirmed against the current product operations source before quoting it externally.
- Some partner systems may have their own credits or API limits. For example, 6sense API credits may be required for certain 6sense APIs and are not necessarily the same as Folloze AI/Data credits.
- Credits should be scoped by entitlement period, included quantity, rollover policy, overage policy, and add-on pricing in the order form.
- Video generation is credit-based where the Video Agent is enabled, and help-center material describes generated video minutes as consuming from the account's FollozeAI allocation.

Recommended sales explanation:

> AI/Data credits are the included usage pool for Folloze's metered AI and data capabilities. They let customers use generation, enrichment, research, and related intelligence features up to the package allowance, with add-on credit packs available when usage grows.

## Modules Included

Modules are the commercial use-case bundles in a package. A package can include one or more modules.

| Module | What it covers |
|---|---|
| ABX | Account-based and demand generation experiences such as 1:1, 1:few, and 1:many account microsites, campaign destinations, account-specific content hubs, and engagement reporting. |
| Events | Digital event hubs, webinar destinations, pre-event and post-event engagement, simulive or video content where enabled, and event engagement reporting. |
| Website Engagement | Website Engager and related website personalization use cases, including embedded Folloze experiences on the corporate site. |
| Sales Enablement | Sales rooms, on-behalf campaigns, prospecting support, seller contact cards, sales dashboards, and sales follow-up based on engagement signals. |

Module names should match the pricing guide and order form. If the product UI uses a slightly different feature name, the customer-facing commercial document should still use the contracted module name.

Current February 2026 customer-facing pricing guide package rows:

| Package | Modules included | Users included | Folloze AI/Data credits included | Integrations | Global Scalability |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Starter | ABX | 2 | 250 | Not included | Not included |
| Professional | ABX + Events | 4 | 1,000 | Included | Not included |
| Premium | ABX + Events + Website Engagement | 8 | 2,000 | Included | Included |
| Enterprise | ABX + Events + Website Engagement + Sales Enablement | 12 | 5,000 | Included | Included |

The pricing deck also describes the commercial build-your-own structure as: choose a platform bundle, choose a customer success option, then add additional licenses, modules, Folloze AI/Data credits, and managed services as needed.

## How To Describe The Package Rows

Use this pattern when explaining the screenshot:

### AI Buyer Experience Platform

This is the core Folloze experience layer. It includes the campaign builder, generator agents, and personalization engine. It is where teams create personalized account experiences, use AI to accelerate copy and content creation, personalize paths for different buyers, publish experiences, and capture engagement.

### Account & Contact Intelligence

This is the intelligence layer. It includes enrichment and research, with Audience Builder marked as roadmap if Product confirms that status. It helps teams understand accounts and contacts well enough to target, personalize, and follow up with relevance.

### Content Engine

This is the content layer. It includes repository and personalization capabilities. It helps teams organize approved content, reuse assets, recommend content, track consumption, and make content relevant to the visitor.

### Integrations

This is the connected-stack layer. It includes MAP, CRM, CDP, and ABM integrations where contracted and configured. It lets Folloze use existing data for targeting and personalization, then send engagement signals back into the systems teams already use.

### Global Scalability

This is the enterprise rollout layer. It includes localization, DAM or governed asset management, data warehouse access, and SSO. It is what allows Folloze to scale across teams, regions, brands, partners, and enterprise security requirements.

### Modules Included

This row should list the commercial modules included in the selected package, such as ABX, Events, Website Engagement, and Sales Enablement.

### Users Included

This row should specify the included user licenses and quantities. Use `Creators included` if the included seats are Creator licenses. List Collaborator, Collaborator Pro, or Associate seats separately if they are included.

### Folloze AI/Data Credits Included

This row should show the included credit allowance for the selected package. Define it as a usage pool for metered AI and data features. The package quantities are confirmed in the February 2026 customer-facing pricing guide, but do not describe exact burn rates unless verified against product operations.
