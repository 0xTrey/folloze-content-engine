# Folloze Authority Campaign Message Briefs

**Prepared:** 2026-08-04
**Status:** Draft briefs only; nothing has been sent
**Required approval:** Exact recipient, exact message, target asset, and supporting evidence must be approved before use

These are review briefs, not mail-merge templates. The sender must add a real relationship, role, and reason for contacting the recipient. Remove any sentence that cannot be supported by a linked public source.

## Partner or integration resource

**Use for:** 6sense, Demandbase, Adobe Marketo, Salesforce, HubSpot, LeanData, ZoomInfo, or Segment after product review
**Recipient role:** Partner marketing, integration product manager, marketplace owner, or documentation owner
**Prerequisite:** The target article is evidence-ready and every joint-workflow statement matches current official documentation
**Do not use when:** Connector support, product behavior, or partner approval is uncertain

**Subject direction:** Review request: practical `[Partner] + Folloze` workflow resource

**Message brief:**

> I prepared a practical guide for teams using `[Partner]` and Folloze together: `[target URL]`. It is intended to answer `[specific shared-user question]`, and I want to make sure the workflow and terminology match the current product documentation before we point anyone to it. Would the appropriate product or partner owner be willing to review the relevant section? If the resource is accurate and genuinely useful for your users, we would also welcome consideration for the most relevant integration or education page. There is no expectation to link if it does not meet your standards.

**Required personalization:**

- exact joint-user problem;
- official documentation reviewed;
- one section requiring confirmation;
- why the recipient owns the review; and
- exact controlled target URL.

**Acceptance before send:**

- no implied endorsement;
- no request for a reciprocal or paid link;
- no unsupported integration behavior;
- recipient is an authorized partner or documentation owner; and
- target page returns 200 and passes the evidence gate.

## Customer evidence or co-authored story

**Use for:** A customer whose outcome is already public or can be explicitly approved
**Recipient role:** Customer marketing, communications, account sponsor, or legal approver
**Prerequisite:** Exact metric, quote, time window, context, and allowed use are documented
**Do not use when:** The proof is internal, pending verification, covered by confidentiality, or lacks an approver

**Subject direction:** Approval request for a source-backed `[Customer]` operating story

**Message brief:**

> We are preparing an educational resource about `[problem or workflow]` and believe the publicly approved `[Customer]` example may help practitioners understand the operating model. Before using it, I would like your team to review the exact language, source, context, and link destination. We will not publish or attribute the result without written approval. If your team prefers to host or reference the approved story on a customer resource page, the supporting Folloze guide is `[target URL]`; that placement is optional and should follow your editorial standards.

**Required personalization:**

- exact approved proof ID or public source;
- proposed customer language;
- metric definition and time window;
- approval owner; and
- exact controlled target URL.

**Acceptance before send:**

- proof is public or written approval is obtained;
- customer controls final wording and logo use;
- no internal-only outcome appears;
- link request is optional and editorially relevant; and
- partner and customer approvals are separated when both are named.

## Editorial, newsletter, podcast, or webinar pitch

**Use for:** A named editor or producer whose existing beat matches the research
**Recipient role:** Editor, reporter, newsletter writer, podcast host, or webinar producer
**Prerequisite:** The benchmark is live with methodology, sample size, date range, limitations, and downloadable summary
**Do not use when:** The asset is unpublished or the finding cannot be reproduced

**Subject direction:** New B2B AI-visibility data: `[one defensible finding]`

**Message brief:**

> We analyzed `[sample and period]` to understand `[specific B2B visibility question]`. The most useful finding for your audience is `[one sourced result]`, with `[one limitation or boundary]`. The methodology and supporting summary are public at `[target URL]`. If this fits your coverage of `[specific beat or recent article]`, I can provide the underlying definitions, a short methodology walkthrough, or an attributable operator perspective. This is offered as source material; coverage and linking are entirely your editorial decision.

**Required personalization:**

- one recent, relevant item from the recipient;
- one result supported by the public dataset;
- sample, period, and limitation;
- why the result matters to that audience; and
- exact benchmark URL.

**Acceptance before send:**

- benchmark is already live;
- methodology is reproducible enough for review;
- finding matches the published data exactly;
- no product superlative is substituted for the finding; and
- no payment, guaranteed placement, or link exchange is proposed.

## Directory, review profile, or marketplace resource

**Use for:** G2, TrustRadius, Capterra, LinkedIn company profile, YouTube, AppExchange, or Adobe Exchange only through the authorized owner
**Recipient role:** Internal corporate profile owner or marketplace listing administrator
**Prerequisite:** Current profile facts and permitted fields are known
**Do not use when:** It would create a duplicate listing, replace Folloze.com as the official site, or bypass the listing owner

**Subject direction:** Review request for Folloze profile and educational-resource fields

**Message brief:**

> Could you review the current `[directory or marketplace]` profile for factual and entity consistency? The official company website should remain `https://www.folloze.com`. If the platform provides a separate resources, education, author, or video field, please consider whether `[controlled target URL]` is appropriate there. No change should be made unless the field permits it and the product, category, and publisher language are current.

**Required personalization:**

- profile or listing URL;
- authorized owner;
- fields that are stale or available;
- exact supporting-resource URL; and
- whether the resulting link is likely follow, nofollow, sponsored, or unknown.

**Acceptance before change:**

- official Folloze.com URL remains intact;
- no duplicate listing is created;
- category and product facts are approved;
- resource field explicitly permits the link;
- the owner makes or approves the edit; and
- the live result is verified and classified accurately in the reporting snapshot.

## Follow-up boundary

One concise follow-up may be drafted after a human-approved first message. Do not automate repeated follow-ups. Close the motion as `closed_no_link` when the recipient declines, the claim cannot be approved, or the placement would require a paid or artificial link arrangement.
